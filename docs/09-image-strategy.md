# Image Strategy

## Goal

Create a coherent visual library that feels like a real UK business.

Do not use random AI-generated images from different styles.

## Image families

### 1. Brand / hero
Modern East London professional environment with subtle digital/technology context.

### 2. Digital & IT
Realistic UK small-business technology environments:
- developer working
- website/product discussion
- IT consultancy
- cybersecurity review

### 3. Consultancy
Realistic customer consultation:
- adviser and customer reviewing documents
- friendly office interaction
- paperwork/tablet without showing sensitive personal data

### 4. Local presence
East London/Barking Road visual context.

### 5. Careers
Professional but natural team collaboration.

## Generation rules

Images should:
- look photographic
- use natural lighting
- avoid impossible interfaces
- avoid excessive blue lighting
- avoid stereotypical “AI office” scenes
- avoid fake logos
- avoid visible personal data
- avoid government branding
- avoid fake certificates

## Real photography priority

If the owner can provide:
- office exterior
- office interior
- team
- staff portraits
- customer-service environment

use those over generated imagery.

AI-generated images should fill gaps rather than represent real employees or premises.

## Required initial image set

Create approximately 8 images:
1. Homepage hero
2. Digital & IT
3. Web/software development
4. Cybersecurity
5. Application assistance
6. Benefits/housing consultation
7. Careers/team
8. East London/local business atmosphere

Use consistent photographic direction across all eight.
