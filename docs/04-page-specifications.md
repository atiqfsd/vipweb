# Page Specifications

## Home

### Goal
Explain the business and route visitors to the correct division quickly.

### Hero
Position VIP e-Services as a technology company with a second, practical consultancy/application-assistance division.

Suggested direction:
“Digital solutions for businesses. Practical application support for local customers.”

Do not copy this verbatim without content review.

### Key CTA
Contact VIP e-Services

### Secondary CTA
Explore services

## Services

Create a visual split between:
- Digital & IT Solutions
- Consultancy & Application Assistance

Each service card must have:
- clear title
- one-sentence explanation
- relevant icon/image
- link where a useful detail page exists

## Service detail page template

1. Breadcrumb
2. Service title
3. Clear explanation of what VIP e-Services helps with
4. Who the service is for
5. What the customer may need
6. How VIP e-Services helps
7. Important disclaimer where appropriate
8. FAQs
9. Related services
10. Contact CTA

Never fabricate:
- fees
- approval rates
- processing times
- government relationships
- success rates
- qualifications
