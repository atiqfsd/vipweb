# Brand Direction

## Existing identity

The current site and physical shopfront use a dark technology-oriented visual language with blue/cyan accents, technology imagery and the VIP e-Services wordmark.

Exact brand guidelines are not currently available.

The initial redesign should therefore preserve recognition while creating a more mature system. Final colours can be adjusted after the owner supplies original brand assets.

## Provisional palette

Use these as working values, not as immutable brand guidelines:

- Deep Navy: #071A2B
- Navy: #0D2940
- Technology Blue: #087EA4
- Cyan Accent: #18A9C8
- White: #FFFFFF
- Off White: #F5F7F8
- Slate Text: #334155
- Muted Text: #64748B
- Border: #DCE4E8

Do not use gradients as the default visual treatment.

## Typography

Preferred direction:

- Headings: modern grotesk/sans-serif with strong readability
- Body: highly readable neutral sans-serif
- Avoid futuristic display fonts
- Avoid excessive uppercase text

Candidate fonts can include Inter, Geist or Manrope, but the final choice should be based on visual testing.

## Logo

Use the existing logo where available. Do not redraw the logo without approval.

## Visual personality

Modern
Professional
Local
Confident
Human
Technical but approachable

## Avoid

- purple AI gradients
- glassmorphism
- neon cyberpunk aesthetics
- floating 3D objects
- generic laptop stock photos
- excessive rounded cards
- excessive shadows
- decorative animations without purpose
- fake dashboards
- fake statistics
- fake customer logos

## Image style

Images should feel like believable UK professional photography:
- natural lighting
- realistic people
- East London context where appropriate
- restrained technology elements
- diverse but natural representation
- no exaggerated futuristic interfaces
