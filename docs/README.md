# VIP e-Services Website Redesign

This folder contains the working product, design, content, SEO and engineering specification for the VIP e-Services website redesign.

## Business positioning

VIP e-Services should be presented primarily as a **Digital & IT company** that also provides **practical consultancy/application assistance** for local customers.

The site must not look like:
- a generic AI SaaS landing page
- a government website
- a law firm
- a fake consultancy with invented credentials
- a template stuffed with stock/AI imagery

The visual direction is **modern UK technology company + trusted local professional service**.

## Core audience

Two audiences should be served equally:

1. Local individuals in East London who need help completing benefits, housing, employment and application processes.
2. Small businesses/organisations in East London that need genuine digital and IT services.

## Geographic focus

East London first. Do not imply nationwide physical coverage unless the business later confirms it.

## Primary contact actions

- Phone
- WhatsApp
- Email
- Visit office
- Contact/enquiry form

There is no appointment-booking flow in the initial release.

## Important content rule

Benefits, housing, work/application and similar services are **application assistance**, not the provision of government benefits or official government services.

Do not claim government affiliation, approval, accreditation or authority unless the client supplies evidence.

## Current site

The current site is a minimal WordPress-style site with Home, About, Services and Contacts. Its copy is heavily generic and primarily presents the business as a digital-services company. The redesign should replace generic marketing copy with specific, useful service information.
