# Remaining Client Questions

These questions should be resolved before final copy and launch.

## Business

- What are the exact opening hours?
- Does the business have a WhatsApp number separate from the phone number?
- Is the listed email still correct?
- What exact legal/registered company name should appear in the footer?
- What year was the business established?

## Digital & IT

- Which digital services are highest priority?
- Does VIP e-Services serve businesses only or individuals too for digital services?
- What technology stacks are actually supported?
- Does cybersecurity include penetration testing directly or through partners?
- What does on-site IT consultancy cover?

## Application assistance

- What does the customer receive for each service?
- Which documents should customers bring?
- Are any services only available in person?
- Are there services that should be removed?
- Are there any service fees?
- Are there official third-party links that should be included?
- Does the business handle document scanning/printing/photocopying?

## Trust

- How many Google reviews are currently available?
- Can the owner provide selected genuine testimonials?
- Are there genuine staff members to feature?
- Are there qualifications/accreditations to display?
- Are there partner organisations?

## Careers

- What kinds of roles should the careers page mention?
- Should speculative CVs be accepted by email?
- Is there a dedicated recruitment email?

## Languages

- Is the full customer service available in all four languages?
- Should the initial launch include translated pages or only prepare the architecture?

## Legal

- What exact disclaimer should be used for benefits/application assistance?
- Does the business provide immigration advice or only application assistance?
- Does the business have any regulated services requiring special wording?

## Content

- Can the owner provide real office photos?
- Can the owner provide staff/team photos?
- Can the owner provide customer-approved photos/testimonials?
