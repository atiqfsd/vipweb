## Services landing page

The services page should clearly split the business into:

### Digital & IT Solutions
Services actually delivered by VIP e-Services.

### Consultancy & Application Assistance
Help completing applications and navigating processes.

## About page

Include:
- company story
- local presence
- what the business does
- customer-first approach
- digital capability
- service categories
- languages
- location
- genuine trust signals

Do not invent employee profiles.

## Careers

Title: Work with VIP e-Services

The page should be ready for future vacancies without inventing current jobs.

Include:
- company culture
- types of work
- future opportunities
- speculative application CTA
- CV/contact instructions

## Resources

Create a useful knowledge hub for SEO and customers.

Examples:
- How to prepare for an application
- What documents should you bring?
- Universal Credit application guidance
- Housing application guidance
- SIA application guidance
- Passport/NICOP preparation
- Small business website basics

Every article must be fact-checked and should clearly distinguish general information from official government guidance.

## Contact

Include:
- address
- phone
- email
- WhatsApp
- enquiry form
- map
- opening hours once confirmed
- languages
- clear directions/visit message
