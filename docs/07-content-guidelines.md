# Content Guidelines

## Tone

Professional
Clear
Human
Helpful
Confident
Local

Use UK English.

## Avoid

- “revolutionary”
- “cutting-edge” everywhere
- “seamless” repeatedly
- “unlock your potential”
- “elevate your business”
- “premium solutions” as filler
- “AI-powered” unless a real service depends on it
- fake urgency
- exaggerated claims

The current site overuses generic premium/elite language. Replace this with concrete explanations.

## Writing principles

Say what the company does.
Say who it helps.
Say what the customer should do next.

Prefer:
“We help small businesses in East London build and improve their websites.”

Over:
“We deliver transformative digital experiences that unlock unparalleled growth.”

## Trust

Trust must come from:
- real reviews
- real location
- real company information
- real service descriptions
- real people
- transparent process

Never invent:
- testimonials
- staff
- credentials
- statistics
- awards
- partnerships
- customer logos

## Government/application services

Always distinguish between:
- official government service
- application assistance
- general information

Do not imply VIP e-Services makes decisions or guarantees outcomes.

## CTA language

Prefer:
- Call us
- WhatsApp us
- Visit our office
- Send an enquiry
- Ask us about this service

Avoid:
- Start your journey
- Unlock now
- Get instant access
- Claim your success

## FAQ content

FAQs should answer real objections:
- What do I need to bring?
- Can you help me complete the form?
- Do I need an appointment?
- Where are you located?
- What languages do you support?
- Can I contact you by WhatsApp?
- Is this an official government service?

Only answer what the business has verified.
