# Structured Data

## Recommended

### Organization
Use the business name, URL, logo, phone and address.

### LocalBusiness
Use where appropriate for the physical local business.

### WebSite
Include site identity.

### BreadcrumbList
Use on inner pages.

### Service
Use on genuine service pages.

### FAQPage
Use only when FAQs are visibly present and genuinely answer customer questions.

## Reviews

Only use review/rating structured data when it meets Google's current requirements.

Do not invent ratings or aggregate scores.

Do not create review markup for testimonials that are not validly sourced.

## Government-related services

Do not use structured data to imply official government status.
