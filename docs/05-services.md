## Service-copy rule

Never write “we provide Universal Credit” or equivalent wording.

Prefer:
- “We can help you complete your Universal Credit application.”
- “We provide application assistance.”
- “We can help you understand the information and documents needed for your application.”

Exact wording should be reviewed for each regulated/government-related service.

## Service hierarchy
